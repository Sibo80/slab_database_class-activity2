1. Transaction Processing System

A Transaction Processing System records daily business transactions. At SuperMart SA, the TPS is used at checkout counters and in the inventory warehouse

At the checkout counters, it records:

Products purchased by customers
Prices and quantities of products sold
Payments made by customers
Receipts issued after purchases

In the inventory warehouse, it records:

New stock received from suppliers
Products moved in and out of the warehouse
Stock levels and damaged products
Products that need to be reordered

The TPS is important because it keeps accurate records of daily transactions and helps the company manage its sales and stock

2. Management Information Systemsand Decision Support Systems

Management Information System

An MIS collects and processes business data and turns it into reports that managers can use

At SuperMart SA, store managers can use an MIS to view weekly sales reports, compare sales between weeks, check which products sell the most, and monitor stock levels

This helps managers understand the store's performance and plan their activities

Decision Support System

A DSS analyses information to help managers make decisions and solve problems

If sales suddenly drop, a DSS can help store management compare sales data, check customer demand, review product prices, and consider different promotional options

For example, the system can help managers decide whether to offer discounts on products that are not selling well

The DSS supports decision-making by helping managers compare options before choosing the best action

3. Executive Support System 

An Executive Support System provides information to senior managers and executives to support strategic decisions

At SuperMart SA, the CEO can use an ESS to view:

Overall sales and profit reports from different stores
Competitor information, including prices and market trends
Long-term sales forecasts
Business growth and performance trends
Information about possible new store locations

This information helps the CEO understand the company's overall performance and make long-term business decisions

4. Knowledge Work Systems and Office Automation Systems 

Knowledge Work System

A KWS supports skilled workers who use specialised knowledge to create, analyse, design, or develop new ideas.

At SuperMart SA, designers can use computer-aided design (CAD) software to create store layouts and plan where shelves, products, and checkout counters should be placed.

This helps them develop and improve store designs

Office Automation System

An OAS helps employees complete everyday office tasks and communication

At SuperMart SA, employees can use Microsoft Word to prepare documents and Microsoft Outlook to send emails and organise meetings

These tools save time and improve communication and administration

5. IT Landscape and Legacy Systems

Why does connecting legacy systems to modern cloud applications increase complexity?

Legacy systems are older computer systems,Modern cloud applications run on internet-based platforms

Connecting the two can increase complexity because they may use different technologies, data formats, and communication methods. Some older systems may also be difficult to update or connect to newer applications

for example, SuperMart SA may have an old inventory system that cannot easily share stock information with a modern cloud-based sales application

The company may need additional software, system updates, and security measures to make the systems work together

Two ways SuperMart SA can save costs using an IT Landscape Diagram

1. Tracking End-of-Life assets

An IT Landscape Diagram can show which computers, servers, and software systems are approaching or have reached the end of their supported life

This helps SuperMart SA identify outdated equipment and software that may be expensive to maintain. The company can plan replacements and avoid unnecessary repair and maintenance costs

2. Standardisation

An IT Landscape Diagram can help the company identify systems and software that perform the same functions

SuperMart SA can standardise its technology by using fewer types of software, hardware, and applications across its stores

This can reduce software licensing costs, training expenses, maintenance costs, and the complexity of managing different systems


Information systems help SuperMart SA collect, process, store, and share information. TPS records daily transactions, MIS provides reports, DSS supports decision-making, ESS supports strategic planning, KWS assists specialised workers, and OAS automates office tasks

Managing the IT landscape also helps the company connect its systems, identify outdated technology, and reduce costs
